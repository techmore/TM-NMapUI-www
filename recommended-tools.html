NetSpot WiFi Analyzer Wi-Fi survey and analyzer Heatmaps, signal troubleshooting, channel planning, site surveys Free tier; paid Home, Pro, and Enterprise editions. Best fit when a vulnerability scan finds devices, but you also need to understand wireless coverage and weak spots. NetSpot
Wireshark Packet analyzer Deep packet capture, protocol debugging, incident triage Free and open source Essential when a scan identifies a service but you need to inspect traffic behavior or prove what is happening on the wire. Wireshark
Angry IP Scanner Fast IP discovery Quick ping sweeps and lightweight host discovery Free and open source Useful for quick LAN discovery before deciding what to scan more deeply with NMapUI. Angry IP
Advanced IP Scanner Windows LAN scanner Quick Windows-friendly network discovery and remote access shortcuts Free A simple helpdesk-friendly option for Windows shops that need fast device visibility. Advanced IP Scanner
Fing Desktop Home and small office network scanner Device recognition, basic monitoring, network history Desktop is free for personal use; paid Starter, Premium, and Professional subscriptions available Good for non-specialists who need understandable device discovery without enterprise monitoring overhead. Fing
Lansweeper Asset inventory Hardware/software inventory, asset discovery, compliance support Free up to 100 assets; Starter from $199/month billed annually with 2,000 assets Fills the asset management gap that scan tools do not solve by themselves. Lansweeper
Domotz Network monitoring MSP-friendly device monitoring, SNMP, alerts, remote management $1.50 per managed device/month; free discovery for unmanaged devices Transparent pricing and simple deployment make it practical for small business and MSP monitoring. Domotz
Auvik Network management Topology mapping, configuration backups, MSP monitoring Custom quote; priced around core network infrastructure devices Strong when automatic network maps and switch/router/firewall visibility matter more than raw scan output. Auvik
Paessler PRTG Sensor-based monitoring SNMP, bandwidth, server, application, and infrastructure monitoring Sensor-based subscription; Paessler notes most devices need roughly 5-10 sensors A mature monitoring platform for teams that want broad metrics and alerting in one product. PRTG
WiFi Explorer macOS Wi-Fi analyzer Channel, BSSID, RSSI, noise, and wireless troubleshooting on Mac Paid Mac app; pricing varies by edition and store Fast macOS-native alternative when you need wireless diagnostics without a full survey workflow. WiFi Explorer
Ekahau Connect Professional Wi-Fi design Enterprise Wi-Fi design, validation, and survey work Quote/reseller pricing; professional bundles are commonly hardware plus annual subscription The higher-end choice when Wi-Fi design accuracy, validation gear, and professional reporting are required. Ekahau

A pragmatic stack for a small IT team
